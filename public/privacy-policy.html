Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Arakat is built around one principle: your data stays on your device. This policy applies to the arakat.app website and all apps published by Arakat.

What we collect

Nothing. Arakat does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. There are no accounts, no sign-ups and no forms that capture personal information.

Cookies and tracking

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics scripts, advertising pixels, or third-party tracking of any kind. Your preferences (language, theme) are stored locally in your browser using localStorage and never leave your device.

Third-party services

The website loads fonts from Google Fonts. This means your browser makes a request to Google's servers to download the font files. If you prefer to avoid this, you can disable web fonts in your browser settings.

Children

Arakat does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, the "Last updated" date above will be updated.
